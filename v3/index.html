Our Kitchen

Soul food reimagined with a healthy twist

At Springreens, our menu combines the best of classic soul food with healthy options that nourish the body without compromising on flavor. Each dish is thoughtfully prepared with the finest ingredients for a nutritious, satisfying meal every time.

Popular dishes include our signature Southern-style greens, savory mac & cheese, and our ever-popular cornbread that melts in your mouth — plus a variety of vegan options, so there's a perfect plate for everyone.
